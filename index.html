Stickman Studio Publisher

A private publishing and analytics tool for two Czech YouTube channels: Tvýma očima and Neotvírej.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service YouTube Terms of Service Google Privacy Policy

Uses YouTube API Services

This tool uses the YouTube Data API v3 and the YouTube Analytics API. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos and Shorts to the owner's own channels.
  • Sets titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, playlists and scheduled publish times.
  • Reads the owner's own YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources) to plan future videos.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. The tool runs locally on the owner's computer, is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else, and only accesses channels the owner signs in with through Google OAuth 2.0.
